## Description (4000)

```
Know what's left this month.

Income Tracker is a simple, private money app for people who want to know where their money goes without handing over their bank login. Add your pay, log what you spend, and the app shows what's left, where it went, and what you can afford next.

LOG A SPEND IN ONE LINE
Type "Tesco 23.40" and it's done. The app fills in the category from your past entries and remembers your usual amounts, so regular spends take a second.

IMPORTS THAT SORT THEMSELVES
Import a bank statement as CSV, OFX or QIF, paste transactions, or import from a screenshot. Each row is matched to a category from the rules the app learns as you go, and likely duplicates are flagged before anything is added. You review it all before it's saved.

NO BANK LOGIN NEEDED
There's no open banking connection and nothing to authorise. You decide what goes in.

EVERY ACCOUNT, ONE NET WORTH
Track current accounts, savings, credit cards and loans side by side. Debt payments move both accounts on the day they happen, so your net worth is always right.

SEE WHERE IT ALL WENT
Insights show your savings rate, spending by category and the payments that repeat every month, so forgotten subscriptions stand out.

GOALS THAT FIT YOUR BUDGET
Put your goals in order and the app shares out your monthly surplus between them, with a timeline showing when each one will be done.

ON YOUR HOME SCREEN AND WITH SIRI
Widgets show what's left this month and your net worth. Use Siri or Shortcuts to add an expense or check your surplus without opening the app.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Lock the app with Face ID. Your ledger is stored on your device, and if you sign in with Apple, Google or email it syncs to your other devices and the Income Tracker web app.

Made in the UK. Works in your own currency.
```
