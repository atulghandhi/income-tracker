Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2025  ·  Last updated: June 12, 2026

The Income Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the service:

  • Account data — when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google.
  • Financial ledger data — the income, expense, debt, and savings entries you create in the app. This data is stored locally in your browser and, if you are signed in, synced to our cloud database (Supabase) so you can access it across devices.
  • Usage data — standard server logs including IP address, browser type, and page requests. We do not use third-party analytics trackers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate The Income Tracker service.
  • To sync your ledger across devices when you are signed in.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Ledger data for signed-in users is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database hosted in the United States. Data is protected by row-level security policies so only you can access your records. Local-only users' data stays entirely in their browser's localStorage and never leaves their device.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google OAuth — used for sign-in only. We do not access your Google Drive, Gmail, or any other Google data.
  • Supabase — our backend database and authentication provider.
  • Vercel — our hosting platform.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to store your ledger state locally and an authentication session cookie set by Supabase when you sign in. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

The Income Tracker is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do we will update the "last updated" date above. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello@theincometracker.com, or contact us here.
