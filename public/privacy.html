Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2025  ·  Last updated: October 29, 2026

The Income Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy covers the website and web app at theincometracker.com and the Income Tracker iPhone app. It explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the service:

  • Account data — signing in is optional. If you sign in with Apple, Google or email, we receive your email address (and, from Google, your name and profile picture). If you use Sign in with Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive only Apple's private relay address.
  • Financial ledger data — the income, expense, account, debt and savings entries you create or import. This data is stored on your device (in your browser on the web, or in the app's storage on iPhone) and, if you are signed in, synced to our cloud database (Supabase) so you can access it across devices.
  • Usage data — standard server logs including IP address, browser type and page requests. The web app may send anonymous usage events (for example "an entry was added") to PostHog to help us improve the product; these never include amounts, merchants, categories or balances, and are not sent if your browser has Do Not Track turned on. The iPhone app does not include any analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate The Income Tracker service.
  • To sync your ledger across your devices when you are signed in.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. Data Storage

Ledger data for signed-in users is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database hosted in the United States. Data is protected by row-level security policies so only you can access your records. If you do not sign in, your data stays entirely on your device — in your browser's localStorage on the web, or in the app's on-device storage on iPhone — and never leaves it.

On iPhone, Face ID is used only by iOS to unlock the app; we never receive your face data. The Home Screen widgets read a summary of your ledger from storage shared on your device with the app.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Sign in with Apple and Google sign-in — used for sign-in only. We do not access your iCloud, Google Drive, Gmail or any other Apple or Google data.
  • Supabase — our backend database and authentication provider.
  • Vercel — our website hosting platform.
  • PostHog — anonymous product usage events from the web app, as described above.
  • Anthropic — if you ask the web app to suggest categories for imported transactions, the bank descriptions of those rows (not amounts, dates or balances) are sent to Anthropic's Claude model to suggest a category.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

On the web we use browser localStorage to store your ledger locally and an authentication session set by Supabase when you sign in. On iPhone, your sign-in session is kept in the iOS Keychain. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

6. Data Retention and Deleting Your Account

Your cloud data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and all of its cloud data at any time:

  • In the iPhone app — open Settings, then tap Delete account. Your account, cloud ledger and any connected bank-feed records are deleted immediately. If you use the same sign-in for another of our apps, the sign-in itself is kept for that app; everything belonging to The Income Tracker is still deleted.
  • By email — write to us at the address below and we will delete your account within 30 days.

Data stored only on your device is removed when you clear it in the app, clear your browser data, or delete the iPhone app. Feedback you have sent us is kept but is no longer linked to your account.

7. Children's Privacy

The Income Tracker is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including under UK and EU GDPR) you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. You can export your ledger at any time from Settings, and delete your account as described above. To exercise any other right, contact us at the address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do we will update the "last updated" date above. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
