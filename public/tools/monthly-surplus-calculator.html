Frequently asked questions

What is a monthly surplus?
It is the money left after you subtract your expenses from your income. It is the number that decides how fast you can save or pay off debt.
What is a good savings rate?
20% or more is strong. Even 10% builds a buffer over time. Anything negative means you are spending more than you earn.
