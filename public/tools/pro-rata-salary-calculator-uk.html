Pro rata salary calculator (UK)

By The Income Tracker · Updated 16 September 2026

The short answer

Pro rata pay is the full-time salary multiplied by your hours divided by the full-time hours. A £30,000 job on 37.5 hours pays £18,000 for 22.5 hours a week, or £1,500 a month before tax. Statutory holiday is 5.6 weeks of your own working hours, so 22.5 hours a week gives 126 hours a year.

The formula

Pro rata salary = full-time salary × (your weekly hours ÷ full-time weekly hours). The hourly rate is the full-time salary divided by (full-time hours × 52). Holiday is 5.6 weeks of your own hours, capped at 28 days’ worth for people who work more than five days a week.

Example: £30,000 full time, 22.5 hours a week

22.5 ÷ 37.5 is 0.6, so the pro rata salary is £18,000: £1,500 a month or £346.15 a week before deductions. The hourly rate is £15.38. Holiday is 5.6 × 22.5, or 126 hours a year, which is 16.8 of your 7.5-hour days.

Things that are not pro rata

  • The Personal Allowance and tax bands are not reduced for part-time work, so part-time earnings are taxed relatively lightly.
  • Some benefits in kind, such as a fixed phone allowance, may be given in full. Check the contract.
  • Bank holidays: a part-timer working Monday to Wednesday gets a pro rata share of bank holidays, not just the ones that fall on their days. Employers must handle this fairly.

Frequently asked questions

How do I work out a pro rata salary?
Multiply the full-time salary by your hours and divide by the full-time hours. £30,000 × 22.5 ÷ 37.5 = £18,000.
What does 0.6 FTE mean?
Sixty percent of full-time hours and, normally, sixty percent of the full-time salary. On a 37.5-hour week that is 22.5 hours.
How much holiday do part-time workers get in the UK?
The same 5.6 weeks as full-time staff, in their own hours. Someone working 20 hours a week is entitled to 112 hours a year.
Is the pro rata salary before or after tax?
Before. Tax, National Insurance, pension and student loan repayments are deducted from it. The Personal Allowance is not reduced for part-time work.
