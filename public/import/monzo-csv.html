How to export your Monzo statement to CSV

By The Income Tracker · Updated 16 June 2026

In short

Log in to the Monzo app (iOS or Android), open the account, choose a date range and export the transactions as a CSV file. Then import that file into The Income Tracker to track your income and spending — free, with no bank login.

A CSV is just a spreadsheet of your transactions. Once you have it, The Income Tracker reads the dates, descriptions and amounts automatically, so you can fill a whole month in seconds without typing anything in by hand.

Export Monzo transactions as CSV, step by step

  1. Open the Monzo app and find your transaction feed.
  2. Tap Manage next to the account (or the three dots on a business card).
  3. Tap Bank statements.
  4. Choose the date range you want.
  5. Select CSV as the format and generate the statement.
  6. Share or save the file, then import it into The Income Tracker.

Monzo also offers PDF and QIF — pick CSV for the cleanest import.

What happens after you import the CSV

Drop the file into The Income Tracker and you'll see every transaction laid out in a month-by-month ledger. Drag transactions into categories, mark recurring items, and watch your surplus update live. Money moved between your own accounts can be auto-skipped so transfers don't double-count.

Frequently asked questions

Can I export Monzo transactions to CSV for free?
Yes. Monzo lets you download your own transactions as a CSV file at no cost from the Monzo app (iOS or Android). You then import that file into a budgeting tool such as The Income Tracker.
Do I need to connect or log in to my bank to use The Income Tracker?
No. There is no open-banking connection and you never enter bank credentials into The Income Tracker. You download the CSV from Monzo yourself and drop it into the app, which runs entirely in your browser.
Is my Monzo data kept private?
Yes. The Income Tracker is local-first: your imported transactions are stored in your own browser and are never uploaded to a server unless you choose to sign in to sync across devices.
