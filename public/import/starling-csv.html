How to export your Starling Bank statement to CSV

By The Income Tracker · Updated 16 June 2026

In short

Log in to the Starling app or web banking, open the account, pick a date range and export as CSV. Then import that file into The Income Tracker and track your money for free, with no bank login.

Export Starling Bank transactions as CSV

  1. Open the Starling app and go to the account you want.
  2. Tap the account name, then Account or Account information.
  3. Tap Statements (or Export statement).
  4. Choose your date range and select CSV as the format.
  5. Save the file and drop it into The Income Tracker.

Starling lets you export specific date ranges as CSV, which is handy for filling one month at a time.

What happens after you import

Drop the file in and every transaction lands in a month-by-month ledger. Drag items into categories, flag the recurring ones, and watch your surplus update as you go. Money moved between your own accounts gets auto-skipped, so transfers never double-count.

Frequently asked questions

Can I export Starling Bank transactions to CSV for free?
Yes. Starling Bank lets you download your own transactions as a CSV file at no cost from the Starling app or web banking. You then import that file into a budgeting tool such as The Income Tracker.
Do I need to connect or log in to my bank to use The Income Tracker?
No. There is no open-banking connection and you never enter bank credentials into The Income Tracker. You download the CSV from Starling Bank yourself and drop it into the app, which runs entirely in your browser.
Is my Starling Bank data kept private?
Yes. The Income Tracker is local-first. Your imported transactions stay in your own browser and are never uploaded to a server, unless you choose to sign in to sync across devices.
