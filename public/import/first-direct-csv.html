How to export your first direct statement to CSV

By The Income Tracker · Updated 16 November 2026

In short

Yes, you can. Log in to first direct Online Banking in a desktop browser (the app only offers PDF statements), open the account, pick a date range and export as CSV. The mobile app cannot do it. Then drop that file into The Income Tracker to see it as a budget, free and with no bank login.

CSV export?
Yes, and it is free.
From the mobile app?
No. The app only offers PDF statements, and the option is often hidden on phone browsers.
Where
First direct Online Banking in a desktop browser (the app only offers PDF statements).
How far back?
One year per download, going back up to six years on current accounts.
Formats
CSV, QIF (Quicken and Microsoft Money flavours), JSON and PDF.
Time needed
About three minutes.

Export first direct transactions as CSV

  1. Log on to Online Banking in a desktop browser. The download option does not show in the app and is often hidden on phones.
  2. Select the account to open its transactions page.
  3. Use the download option on the transactions page and set a date range of up to one year.
  4. Choose CSV. QIF and JSON are also offered.
  5. Download, then repeat for each earlier year you need. Current accounts go back up to six years.
  6. Import each file into The Income Tracker.

first direct's own help pages only describe PDF statements, so the label on the download option may differ from what you expect. Look on the transactions page rather than under Statements. Credit card exports cover a shorter period, around six months.

Frequently asked questions

Does first direct still offer QIF?
Users report it disappeared in one redesign and came back later. CSV has been consistently available on the desktop site, which is the format the tracker prefers anyway.
Is it free to export first direct transactions to CSV?
Yes. first direct lets you download your own transactions as a file at no cost from first direct Online Banking in a desktop browser (the app only offers PDF statements). You then import that file into a budgeting tool such as The Income Tracker, which is also free.
What can I do with my first direct CSV once I have it?
Open it in a spreadsheet to read it, or import it into The Income Tracker to see it as a month-by-month budget: income, spending by category, recurring bills and what is left over. Importing takes a few seconds and you can add next month's file on top without creating duplicates.
Do I need to connect or log in to my bank to use The Income Tracker?
No. There is no open-banking connection and you never enter bank credentials into The Income Tracker. You download the file from first direct yourself and drop it into the app, which runs entirely in your browser.
Is my first direct data kept private?
Yes. The Income Tracker is local-first. Your imported transactions stay in your own browser and are never uploaded to a server, unless you choose to sign in to sync across devices.
