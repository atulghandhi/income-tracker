How to export your Lloyds Bank statement to CSV

By The Income Tracker · Updated 16 November 2026

In short

Yes, you can. Log in to Lloyds Internet Banking on a computer (the app cannot export CSV), open the account, pick a date range and export as CSV. The mobile app cannot do it. Then drop that file into The Income Tracker to see it as a budget, free and with no bank login.

CSV export?
Yes, and it is free.
From the mobile app?
No. The Lloyds app cannot export CSV. Use Internet Banking on a computer.
Where
Lloyds Internet Banking on a computer (the app cannot export CSV).
How far back?
The last 12 months, 150 transactions per file. Narrow the dates and export again for more.
Time needed
About three minutes.

Export Lloyds Bank transactions as CSV

  1. Log in to Lloyds Internet Banking on a computer.
  2. Open the account and find the Statement options box above the transactions.
  3. Click it and choose Export transactions (CSV, QIF).
  4. Set your date range and choose CSV.
  5. The file downloads as exportStatements-xx.csv, ready to import into The Income Tracker.

Lloyds caps each export at the last 12 months and 150 transactions. Hit the cap? Export again with a narrower date range and import each file.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free to export Lloyds Bank transactions to CSV?
Yes. Lloyds Bank lets you download your own transactions as a file at no cost from Lloyds Internet Banking on a computer (the app cannot export CSV). You then import that file into a budgeting tool such as The Income Tracker, which is also free.
What can I do with my Lloyds Bank CSV once I have it?
Open it in a spreadsheet to read it, or import it into The Income Tracker to see it as a month-by-month budget: income, spending by category, recurring bills and what is left over. Importing takes a few seconds and you can add next month's file on top without creating duplicates.
Do I need to connect or log in to my bank to use The Income Tracker?
No. There is no open-banking connection and you never enter bank credentials into The Income Tracker. You download the file from Lloyds Bank yourself and drop it into the app, which runs entirely in your browser.
Is my Lloyds Bank data kept private?
Yes. The Income Tracker is local-first. Your imported transactions stay in your own browser and are never uploaded to a server, unless you choose to sign in to sync across devices.
